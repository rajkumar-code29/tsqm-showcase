The King of the Oceans Egg

Edition 5 of 250 · 1977

Sterling silver and gold, guilloché enamelled in deep blue. Height 210mm.

Inside

Neptune in gold and silver, trident in one hand, a roped miniature pearl egg in the other.

Provenance

Commissioned by the St Petersburg Collection. Held by the charity for the benefit of the restoration.

The Clyde Egg

Edition 12 of 150 · 1983

Enamel over an engine-turned ground, mounted on a silver-gilt stand. Height 185mm.

Inside

A miniature paddle steamer in gold, on a ground of lapis.

Condition

Enamel sound throughout. Minor rubbing to the gilding at the foot, consistent with handling.
